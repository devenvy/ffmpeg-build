Linux x64 runtime programs in both library linking modes

The LGPL-2.1-or-later v4l libraries remain independently linked libraries.
The following GPL-2.0-only decoder programs are separate executables invoked
through upstream libv4lconvert fork/pipe framing. They are not linked into
FFmpeg or the libraries. The accompanying COPYING contains their GPLv2 text;
the exact pinned source archive and recipe contain their complete sources
and reproducible build. Other platforms retain their existing packaging.
